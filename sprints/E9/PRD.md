# Sprint E9 PRD: Transaction Costs and Capacity

Sprint E9, 2026-09-21. Tier 3. The sprint that answers the PM question
"does the theoretical alpha survive implementation, and at what AUM does it
stop?" The seed alpha is the E8 synthetic alpha with a known IC, labeled as
such, because no real signal passed RG-Signal.

## Research framing, copied verbatim from the roadmap

- Academic question: How do transaction costs enter the portfolio problem, and how does the optimal trade change from full rebalancing to partial adjustment?
- Practitioner question: Does the theoretical alpha survive implementation, and at what AUM does it stop?
- Research question: What is the capacity of the seed book, and how much turnover is worth its cost?

Objective. Make construction cost-aware: a cost model, a turnover-penalized
optimizer, trade scheduling, and the capacity curve of the seed book.

## The controlled input

The alpha is the E8 synthetic alpha with a known IC (rho = 0.02, 0.05,
0.10). Capacity is a function of the assumed IC, not a single number, so
the capacity curve and the halving AUM are reported at each rho. This
book's real capacity is undefined because no signal passed RG-Signal; the
table answers the useful question instead: what IC a strategy needs to
support a given AUM under these costs.

## Cost model, formulas with inputs and outputs labeled

- Corwin-Schultz half-spread from daily high-low ranges, probe bid-ask
  availability first. INPUT: high, low, close. OUTPUT: the half-spread
  estimate per name.
- Square-root impact: cost = k * sigma * sqrt(|delta w| * AUM / ADV), with
  ADV the average daily dollar volume and k the impact coefficient, stated
  with its uncertainty.
- Commissions: a flat per-share or per-dollar rate, stated.
- Borrow on shorts: a rate per year on short notional, stated.
- Total cost of a trade delta w: half_spread * |delta w| + impact +
  commission, with borrow as a holding cost.

## Constructions

- Cost model from the price panel (high/low/volume are stored).
- Turnover-penalized optimization: trade-toward-target versus full
  rebalance, the first-order solution of the cost-aware objective.
- Rebalance-frequency trade-off: net IR against rebalance cadence.
- Capacity curve: net Sharpe against AUM; the AUM at which net Sharpe
  halves, stored per rho with its sensitivity to the impact coefficient k.

## The memo

Transaction Cost and Capacity Analysis, written for a senior quant or risk
manager: the cost model with parameters and their uncertainty, cost curves
by size decile, the turnover versus IR frontier, the recommended cadence
and turnover budget, the capacity curve with the halving AUM and its
sensitivity to k, the stored numbers, the practitioner conclusion, and a
section titled "What would falsify this?". A negative verdict is a complete
deliverable.

## What would falsify the sprint's conclusion

- Net Sharpe is not monotone in AUM: the cost model is mis-specified.
- The turnover penalty destroys ex-ante IR: the signal horizon is wrong for
  the cost regime.
- Realized fills in E11 outside the model's band: parameters are
  recalibrated and the capacity curve reissued.
